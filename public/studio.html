A shape becomes a current.

Start with a look. Choose a preset, then try a built-in shape or import an SVG. Only the SVG’s visible silhouette is used; its original colours are replaced by your palette.

Make it liquid. Use larger eddies, gentle turbulence, and a longer trail lifetime. Viscosity smooths the motion; diffusion softens the colour ribbons. Flow speed moves the light, while evolution speed changes the current itself.

Make it yours. Size and position move the source. Drag in the preview to stir. Changing a shape clears the flow; changing motion lets the existing trails evolve.

SVG preparation. Keep the background transparent. Outline text and include a viewBox. Paths, strokes, transforms, and holes work. External images, scripts, and linked fonts are excluded.

Save your work. Controls are remembered in this browser. Save a project to keep your SVG and settings together, or export the current frame at up to 4K. Render quality controls the detail in the moving trails. Choose High or Maximum before a large export. Drag the divider beside the controls to resize the live preview, or use Expand preview. Export windows can also be resized; Fit shows the whole image; the image zoom menu goes up to 6400%, with dragging to pan. Live-preview zoom also reaches 6400% and leaves exports unchanged.
